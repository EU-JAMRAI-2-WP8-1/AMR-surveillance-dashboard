### Improving patient outcome by supporting data driven national empiric treatment guidance for common infections

<i class="fa fa-handshake"></i> **Policy commitment:** Support EU Council Recommendation on AMR (13) by working towards development of EU guidelines for treatment of common infections recognizing that resistance patterns can vary in different regions [condensed].

**The European landscape for national empiric treatment guidance reveals key trends and strengths but also significant gaps.**

### Major trends include:

- The majority of European countries have national empiric treatment guidance in place for common infections. STRENGTH

- Sixteen out of 28 countries report using routine AST data for writing their treatment guidance. STRENGTH 

- Only 61% of countries have national treatment guidance for bloodstream infections. GAP

### Proposed actions:

- Support countries without national empiric treatment guidance to begin the process of drafting guidance.

- Support countries to use routine AST data when updating or generating guidelines. If local data is insufficient, aid in establishing networks for shared regional data.

### Why does having data driven national empiric treatment guidance matter?

Empiric treatment guidance lays the foundation for harmonized and effective patient treatment. It can be locally adapted if needed. It is a powerful way of using surveillance data to benefit patients and ensure that antibiotic treatment failures are avoided. It is a necessary first step towards harmonized EU guidelines.


**Note:** Having data-driven national empiric treatment guidance in place does not guarantee that they are implemented and widely used. However, having guidance in place lays a necessary foundation for AMR stewardship activities.
