# Welcome to <span class="jamreye-word">JAMREYE</span> Dashboard - Survey Results

This dashboard presents **current national AMR surveillance information in humans** from the 30 EU-JAMRAI 2 partner countries. It is designed as a **tool for national and international AMR experts** and policymakers to **explore, compare, and analyse surveillance information** across Europe.
