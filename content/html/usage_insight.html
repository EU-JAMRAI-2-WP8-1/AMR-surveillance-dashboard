The Insights present our interactive and comparative analysis of the survey data, built around the three policy commitments of the 2023 Council Recommendation on AMR. Each insight combines an interactive figure with its key findings.

For project details and contacts, please click the Info button at the bottom of the menu bar.

How to Use the Insights
  1. Choose an insight with the buttons in the left panel.
  2. The figure (left) shows the results by country. Hover over it for details.
  3. The key findings (right) summarise what the figure shows.
  4. Click countries on the figure to highlight them and compare them side by side in the table below it.

Filters

Narrow the figures by:

  • Culture material: Filter by specimen type (e.g., blood, urine).
  • Pathogens: Focus on specific bacterial pathogens.
  • Resistances: Select specific antibiotic resistances.
Countries

Click a country to cycle it through three states:

  • Shown: the country appears on the figures.
  • Selected: the country is also highlighted on the figures and added to the comparison table.
  • Hidden: the country is removed from the figures.

Use Hide all, Show all and Select all to change every country at once.

Tips for Better Navigation
  • Click "Reset filters" to return to the default view.
  • Switch to the Survey results (top of the menu bar) to browse the raw answers to the survey.
  • Click the JAMREYE logo to go back to the welcome page.
  • Click the Help button (top left) to revisit this guide.
